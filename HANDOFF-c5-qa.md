## IMPORTANT facts learned
1. Overlay colours: in every `*_overlay.png` CYAN = Revit only, RED = ours only (the old comment said the opposite; fixed). Numeric findings were never affected.
2. c5-sheets refuted my "cos 29 degrees / grids foreshortened" reading for rst Cross Section 1214432: our grid and level spacing equal Revit's DXF to 0.01 ft; the 0.876 factor came from my eyeballed overlay and the non-uniform registration step. Do not repeat it; measure from the SVG/DXF directly (`qa/vecgeom.py` numbers are reliable, overlay pixel readings are not).
3. Revit's DXF contains no dimension strings (they are only in the PNG) and no hatch/pattern lines: `text-extra-dim` is informational; the PNG tier shows Revit's pattern ink as "missing" on elevations (29154/29181, open item on c5-sheets' side).
4. Sheet DXFs are model-scale per viewport and unusable; sheets use the image tier (and PDF tier if a sheet PDF exists). Whole-sheet long-line/bubble checks are off. The circle-based bubble findings were demoted (fire on door swings).
5. Revit exports are in ~/rvt-research/gt_views/<year>_<file>/ (png/dxf/pdf + index.tsv). New exports from c5-writer: run `./qa-new` then `qa-index`.
